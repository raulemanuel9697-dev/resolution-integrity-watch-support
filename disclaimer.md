# Product boundaries

Resolution Integrity Watch provides a read-only signal for possible disagreement between a Jira issue's status category and Resolution. It does not determine the correct workflow for a team, guarantee detection of all workflow problems, prevent incidents, or repair Jira data.

Review a finding against the issue and the project's workflow before making changes. Jira workflows may intentionally use status and Resolution differently. Scans are limited to 500 issues; a capped scan is labeled in the report.
